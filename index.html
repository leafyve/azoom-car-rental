GOOD TO KNOW

A few more
things to know.

Still have questions? Take a look at these common rental queries.

Can I rent by the day, week or month?

Yes. Choose your dates when booking. Rentals of 7–29 days receive a weekly discount; rentals of 30 days or more receive a monthly discount in this prototype.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Credit cards only. This coursework website demonstrates a credit-card booking and final payment process. It does not process real payments.

Where do I return the car?

Return the EV to the designated car park at your selected branch. An AZoom employee will inspect it for damage.

What happens if the car has damage?

Any assessed damage charge is added to the final bill. The demo booking manager lets you try both no-damage and damage scenarios.
